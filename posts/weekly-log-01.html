Weekly Log #01: Building This Blog from Scratch

Weekly Log

The first thing this week was building this blog from scratch.

What I did

  • Wrote a set of minimalist styles
  • Used placeholder posts to test the typography

Next week

A small takeaway

Building it was more enjoyable than expected. When a page is nothing but text and space, writing becomes a very concrete joy.
