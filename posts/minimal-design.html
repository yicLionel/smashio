Thoughts on Minimalist Design

Design

Minimalism isn't about having nothing — it's about keeping only what's necessary.

Principles of subtraction

  1. For every element removed, ask whether it was truly unnecessary.
  2. Whitespace isn't wasted space; it makes room for attention.
  3. Default typography is often already good — changes need a reason.

Constraints for this site

This blog keeps its style rules deliberately simple:

--bg:    #ffffff;
--fg:    #1a1a1a;
--muted: #8a8a8a;
--line:  #e8e8e8;

Four colors and one typeface. That's it.

True minimalism is using what you have with restraint.

The design may change later, but I hope whatever changes, I remember what this blog looked like at the start: quiet, clear, and only about content.
