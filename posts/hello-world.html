Hello, world. This is the first post I've written on this blog.

The purpose of starting this blog is simple: to give myself a quiet place to record technical notes, reading notes, and scattered thoughts.

Why have a blog

Writing is the best way to think. When you try to explain something clearly, you often realize you haven't fully understood it. A blog is a place to "figure things out".

Writing is thinking; expressing is organizing.

What I plan to write about

  • Technical notes and troubleshooting
  • Reading notes
  • Weekly summaries
  • Occasional random thoughts

About this site

This is a purely static minimalist blog. No database, no framework — just plain HTML and CSS. Its only purpose is to present text cleanly.
