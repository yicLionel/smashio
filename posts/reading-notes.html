Currently Reading: A Book About Time

Reading

I've been reading a book about time. It isn't about the marks on a clock — it's about how we subjectively experience time.

A few points that stuck with me

  • We don't own time; we borrow it.
  • Where your attention goes, your sense of time follows.
  • The more focused you are, the faster time passes; the more aimless, the slower.

What it makes me think of

Recording a day of time and spending it are two completely different experiences. The former makes you aware; the latter just passes.

Time can't be managed. Only priorities can.

I haven't finished the book yet. Maybe I'll write a fuller review when I do.
